# Unscroll screenshot evaluation — 2026-09-04

## Test scope

- Published app: `Screen Time: Unscroll`, Apple ID `6766120727`.
- Market/language: US / English.
- Evidence: live App Store page and Apple lookup, the sibling `doomscroll` repository's marketing/product wiki, seven live 1290×2796 screenshots, six approved real-device source captures, and niceapps.club references.
- Output boundary: strategy and production specification only. No new PNG was rendered.

## Product brief

```yaml
app_name: Screen Time: Unscroll
release_state: published
platform: ios
market: US
language: English
one_line_product: A Screen Time blocker that puts a deliberate pause between an impulse and a distracting app, then turns protected Focus time into visible progress.
primary_audience: iPhone users who repeatedly override ordinary limits during work, study, bedtime, or reflexive feed checks.
problem: Existing limits are easy to dismiss at the exact moment intention is weakest.
core_outcome: Regain a moment of choice before reflexive scrolling begins.
confirmed_features:
  - Protect selected apps, categories, and websites with Apple Screen Time.
  - Run Focus sessions and reusable Routines.
  - Standard Shield adds a 10-second pause.
  - Pro Hard Mode adds a 60-second intervention and final choice.
  - Habits provide replacement actions and reminders.
  - Journey shows completed sessions, protected time, streaks, and milestones.
  - Daily Insights and seven-day Screen Time views expose available patterns.
differentiators:
  - The product focuses on the override moment rather than only scheduling a block.
  - It offers two explicit levels of friction: 10 seconds and 60 seconds.
  - It connects protection to a calm progress and milestone system.
brand_character:
  - calm
  - restorative
  - premium
  - lightly dark
  - non-judgmental
available_raw_screens:
  - Focus setup
  - standard Shield
  - Hard Mode
  - Today / Daily Insights
  - Journey / milestones
  - seven-day Screen Time report
constraints:
  - iPhone-only product UI in English.
  - No unsupported reduction, medical, addiction-cure, or performance claim.
  - Detailed Screen Time reports and protected selections stay on-device; optional minimized cloud backup must not be collapsed into a blanket all-data-on-device claim.
uncertainties:
  - No raw Routines or Habits capture is in the approved App Store capture package.
```

The live App Store page shows version 1.1 released on 2026-08-28. The inspected local marketing context still described 1.1 as awaiting submission and is stale on that point.

## Current set audit

The live order is Outcome → Focus → Insights → Journey → Shield → Hard Mode → Privacy. Its principal weakness is narrative priority: the 10-second Shield and 60-second Hard Mode are the clearest differentiation, but appear only in positions five and six.

Strengths:

- cohesive sanctuary imagery and premium dark presentation;
- real, legible product UI and verified device captures;
- honest mechanism copy and unusually precise 10-second/60-second proof;
- consistent typography, composition language, and export quality;
- meaningful Journey and Screen Time surfaces instead of generic feature cards.

Weaknesses:

- the first phone-free frame can read as meditation/wellness branding and does not prove the product;
- the current first three frames do not establish the differentiating override-moment story;
- Shield and Hard Mode use similar black pause surfaces and need stronger compositional contrast when adjacent;
- the Insights frame contains several competing proof surfaces and small internal text;
- landscape imagery occasionally receives more visual weight than the UI;
- `Your Screen Time Stays Private` is broader than the precise on-device report claim and sits beside App Store privacy labels showing other linked data categories.

## Selected references

### Opal: Screen Time Control — direct product reference

Use: feature proof appears early, the blocking mechanism is unmistakable, and screenshots vary product states rather than repeating one static device. Do not copy its icy sci-fi treatment, ratings/award proof, or unverified outcome language.

### Pebble AI: To-Do & Focus — product-adjacent communication reference

Use: action-first storytelling and large product states make the workflow easy to understand. Its bright, handwritten, playful voice is not an Unscroll brand match.

### mymind — brand-character reference

Use: demonstrates a quiet, private, editorial product voice and varied layouts that still read as one campaign. It is not a functional comparator.

### GO Club — progress-system reference

Use: makes progress and daily metrics visually concrete through oversized proof and varied compositions. Awards, quantified outcomes, and its bright fitness palette are not transferable evidence.

## Recommended narrative

Move from mechanism → setup → stronger boundary → insight → progress → privacy → brand close.

| # | Communication job | Headline | Supporting copy | Source UI / composition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Prove the differentiating mechanism | **Ten seconds between you and the scroll.** | A deliberate pause before a distracting app opens. | Standard Shield capture, largest product surface in the set; keep sanctuary atmosphere subordinate. |
| 2 | Show control and setup | **Choose what pulls you in.** | Protect apps, websites, or categories, then start Focus. | Focus setup with selected-app breakouts; simplify decorative competition around the phone. |
| 3 | Escalate the boundary | **When ten seconds isn't enough.** | Hard Mode adds a full minute before you choose again. | Hard Mode capture plus the final-choice card; distinguish the composition from frame 1. |
| 4 | Reveal patterns | **See the patterns behind the pull.** | Private Screen Time views help you notice how your day unfolds. | Today/Insights capture; keep one primary data proof instead of two competing cards. |
| 5 | Turn protection into progress | **Turn protected time into progress.** | Build streaks and unlock milestones as you keep showing up. | Journey capture and one badge breakout. |
| 6 | Establish precise privacy | **Your Screen Time stays on your iPhone.** | Protected selections and detailed reports remain on-device. | Seven-day Screen Time report; retain the restrained dark composition. |
| 7 | Close on the brand promise | **A pause, built in.** | Screen Time, with intention. | Existing phone-free sanctuary frame, simplified as a closing brand card. |

No Routines/Habits frame is proposed until an approved raw capture exists.

## Visual directions

1. **Sanctuary, product first — recommended.** Preserve the existing landscape/threshold identity, but place a large real product proof in frame one and reduce background dominance across the sequence. This reuses the strongest existing assets and corrects the narrative without losing differentiation.
2. **Obsidian utility.** Use near-black fields, oversized UI, sparse mineral-blue/pearl accents, and very little landscape. Category clarity improves, but the set becomes less distinctive and closer to other productivity tools.
3. **Threshold story.** Contrast the dark automatic-impulse moment with warmer reclaimed-time scenes across the sequence. Emotionally strong, but it requires more new art direction and carries the highest risk of scenery becoming the product.

## Tool and workflow findings

- `search_apps` found useful candidates but its text/category score produced some weak category-only matches. Human visual inspection was still required.
- The first `import_app_store` call failed because Apple returned 403 to the Cloudflare Worker route. Direct Apple lookup from the local machine succeeded. The MCP now retries through a local Apple lookup, covered by a regression test and verified live on Unscroll.
- The workflow found enough evidence without asking the user redundant product questions.
- **Superseded:** the initial evaluation stopped at a production-ready specification because no renderer was available. The final export pass below closes that gap for the Unscroll trial.

## Generated prototype pass

The selected **Sanctuary, product first** direction was exercised with the built-in image-generation model using the approved raw captures as references. The outputs are stored in `output/screenshot-prototypes/unscroll-v1/`, with the prompt set and production boundary recorded in `prototype-notes.md`.

- `01-core-five-contact-sheet.png` proved that the mechanism-first story and existing sanctuary identity can coexist. It also exposed excessive repetition: four frames relied on nearly the same headline-above-straight-phone template.
- `03-core-five-varied-contact-sheet.png` is the preferred core art-direction pass. A targeted revision added threshold crops, UI/card breakouts, asymmetry, and a clearer cool-to-warm rhythm while keeping the five communication jobs intact.
- `02-privacy-brand-contact-sheet.png` produced the strongest ending: a restrained, product-led privacy proof followed by an intentionally emotional, phone-free brand close.

This pass validates the usefulness of generating and scoring contact sheets before production rendering. It does **not** validate final asset generation. The model can change small UI details and text, and the contact sheets are neither individual exports nor `1290 × 2796`. Production output must reconstruct the selected compositions with faithful raw UI and deterministic text/layout.

## Final ASC export pass

The approved captures were recomposed through the deterministic Sharp renderer in `scripts/render-screenshot-set.mjs` using `evals/unscroll-render-manifest.json`. The generated set lives at `output/screenshot-prototypes/unscroll-asc-v1/en-US/APP_IPHONE_67/`.

- Slot: Apple-supported iPhone 6.9-inch portrait size, `1290 × 2796`.
- Deliverables: seven separately named PNG files plus `upload-order.json`.
- Pixel contract: sRGB, three channels, opaque/no alpha.
- Source contract: real supplied captures are resized and composited; generative UI from the contact sheets is not used.
- QA: all seven outputs were opened as one reduced storefront-like contact sheet; Insights and Journey were also inspected individually at full resolution, and their first-pass copy collisions were corrected before the final render.

The final readiness gate is satisfied for this locale/device slot. The contact sheets remain intermediate references and must not be uploaded.
