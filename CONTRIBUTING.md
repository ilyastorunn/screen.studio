# Contributing to niceapps.club

niceapps.club is a community-curated collection of thoughtfully designed apps.

## Add an app

The easiest way to suggest an app is to [open an app request](https://github.com/ilyastorunn/screen.studio/issues/new?template=app-request.yml). Include the app name, category, a short description, App Store URL, and screenshots or image URLs.

You can also fork the repository and open a pull request. Please keep app content accurate, use screenshots you have permission to share, and add one app per pull request.

## App checklist

- The app is available on the App Store.
- The screenshots are your own, supplied by the developer, or publicly shareable.
- The app has a clear category and description.
- URLs are valid and use HTTPS where possible.
